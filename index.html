        How it works

        Local and private

        Everything runs in this tab and clears when you close it. Nothing is uploaded. The only outside request is a Google Font, when you pick one for the credits.

        Score

        PDF.js, from Mozilla, draws each page. Your changes are a list of steps on top of the original PDF, which is never touched. Export PDF builds a new file from that list.

        How the PDF Editor works

        Click the file’s name at the top to open it. Make your changes, then press Done to apply them.

        Arrange

        Drag to reorder. Click to leave out. Insert adds a blank page or another PDF.

        Clean

        Whiten paper and clear specks.

        Re-kilter

        Straighten tilted, slanted, and curved scans.

        Crop

        Trim empty margins around the music. Auto finds the frames for you. Manual lets you drag them yourself.

        Dim

        Soften music before or after a repeat. Draw a box over the music, drag its edges to adjust, and press X to remove it.

        Split spreads

        Turn spreads into single pages.

        Re-size

        Fit every page to one size.

        Watermark

        Print text across chosen pages.

        Audio

        Your browser reads the audio, and the waveform is measured on your device. What you hear while editing is what you export. Long recordings are kept in your browser’s private file storage, so they don’t have to fit in memory.

        How the Audio Editor works

        Click the file’s name at the top to open it. Make your changes, then press Done to apply them. Sound runs through the modules from top to bottom. A lit module is active. Bypass takes out only the open module, and it stays out of the saved sound and the exports until you switch it back on.

        Noise reduction

        Banish hiss, hum, or room tone. Learn from a noise-only selection. Auto finds the stretch for you. Manual lets you pick it yourself.

        Spectra

        Heal or turn down a blip, a hum, or a squeak you can see. Press Draw, then mark a Box, Band, or Moment. Heal fills it in from the sound around it. Reduce turns it down.

        EQ

        Bands can be Bell, Low shelf, High shelf, High-pass, Low-pass, or Notch. EQ Match fits bands to the difference between your recording and a reference.

        Imager

        Widen, narrow, or re-center the sound.

        Level

        Set the finished audio’s volume. Normalize sets a target loudness. Limiter keeps peaks under the ceiling, so nothing clips.

        Fades

        Ease the start and end. Adjust Length or curve type.

        Emulator

        Borrow the sound of another recording. Measure a reference’s tone and loudness, then adjust your EQ, width, and level toward it.

        Page turns

        Each turn is drawn in 3D by your graphics card. Drag a turn into the next one and that one gets pushed along. Hold Shift and click to select several at once. Turns to end packs the turns after the playhead against the end of the video. Add page turn places them in order. In Single page layout, each page gets its own turn, centered in the frame. Style chooses between Turn, Slide, and Cut.

        Video

        Your browser renders and encodes every frame. Unless you pick Real-time recording, the sound is stored losslessly (24-bit) and the video goes into a MOV file. Real-time recording compresses the sound and saves an MP4 or WebM file. On Automatic, if fast export isn’t available, the video is recorded in real time instead.

        Save and load

        Settings stay in this browser. Import / Export saves your settings to a small text file you can load back later.

        About

        ScoreMotion is a modified, independent fork of glisscore, not endorsed by its original author. Source of this version.

        glisscore was made by composer, conductor, and grad student (who should probably be writing music), Franklin D. Hansen.

        glisscore is free and open source under the AGPL-3.0. View on GitHub.

        To support the original Glisscore project, you can support the original author on Ko-fi.

        There’s no pressure: supporting unlocks nothing, and the app is the same for everyone.
